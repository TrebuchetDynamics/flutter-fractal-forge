Fractal Forge is a 100% free, GPU-powered fractal explorer and generative art studio. Explore 966 production fractals — Mandelbrot, Julia, Burning Ship, Newton basins, strange attractors, and ray-marched 3D worlds — with real-time rendering, deep zoom, and full creative control.

No ads. No tracking. No account. Works fully offline.

EXPLORE & CREATE
• 966 fractals: escape-time classics, root-finding basins, strange attractors, IFS, cellular automata, tilings, and 3D forms like Mandelbulb and Mandelbox
• Smooth GPU rendering with deep zoom and pan, plus CPU precision paths for extreme detail
• 60+ color schemes with smooth coloring, orbit traps, distance estimation, and normal-map relief
• Dual Mandelbrot / Julia viewer, curated presets, randomizer, and auto-explore mode
• Formula Lab: write and preview your own custom fractal formulas
• Kaleidoscope and text overlay tools for unique compositions

EXPORT & SHARE
• Save high-resolution PNG images up to 4K, including batch export
• Looper: animate zoom, pan, and parameters, then export looping GIF or MP4 video
• Fractal Music: original soundtracks generated from your fractal’s visual patterns
• Live Fourier spectra of the current render
• Set any fractal as your wallpaper in one tap
• Share images, or links that reopen your exact view

ACCESSIBLE & PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• High contrast, reduced motion, screen-reader labels, and configurable touch targets
• No ads, no tracking, no data collection — the only permission is setting your wallpaper
• Open source (Apache 2.0) and completely free

Perfect for fractal wallpapers, generative art, math and physics education, studying nonlinear systems, or relaxing with infinite mathematical beauty. Every view is unique — and yours to keep.
