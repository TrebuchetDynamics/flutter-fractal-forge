Fractal Forge is a free fractal explorer and generative art studio. Explore over 1,000 fractals — Mandelbrot and Julia sets, Burning Ship, tilings, cellular automata and 3D forms like the Mandelbulb — then turn any view into a wallpaper, a print or a looping GIF.

No ads. No account. No tracking. Explore, create and export offline.

EXPLORE
• Search by name or browse by category: escape-time sets, root-finding basins, strange attractors, IFS, tilings, cellular automata and ray-marched 3D worlds.
• Zoom, pan and rotate 3D fractals with real-time GPU rendering, plus a CPU precision path for extreme deep zoom.
• Open a curated preset, let Randomize pick your next fractal, or let Auto-Explore drift.
• Write and preview your own formulas in Formula Lab.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Pick a palette or build your own, then tune fractal parameters in real time.
• Add kaleidoscope symmetry or a text overlay to compose the frame.
• Switch on the Fourier view for a second reading of the same pattern.

KEEP AND SHARE
• Save & share gathers export, link sharing and wallpaper in one button.
• Export opaque or transparent PNG up to 4K, with Instagram, story and custom sizes, and batch-export every saved preset.
• Set the view as your home or lock-screen wallpaper in one tap.
• Animate zoom, pan and parameters in the animation looper, preview the loop, then export a GIF.
• Share a link that reopens your exact view.

MADE TO BE USABLE
• High contrast, reduced motion, screen-reader labels and adjustable touch targets.
• Free and open source under Apache 2.0. Rendering speed and available zoom depth depend on the fractal and your device.
