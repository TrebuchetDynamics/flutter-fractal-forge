Fractal Forge is a free, open-source fractal explorer and generative art studio. Explore 1,014 production fractals including Mandelbrot and Julia sets, Newton basins, strange attractors, cellular automata, tilings, and ray-marched 3D worlds.

Render in real time with GPU acceleration, deep zoom and pan, curated presets, more than 60 color schemes, orbit traps, distance estimation, and normal-map relief. Create high-resolution PNG images, looping GIF animations, fractal music, kaleidoscopic compositions, and wallpapers.

Fractal Forge works fully offline and includes no advertising, tracking, accounts, analytics, or data collection. Accessibility features include screen-reader labels, high contrast, reduced motion, and configurable touch targets.
