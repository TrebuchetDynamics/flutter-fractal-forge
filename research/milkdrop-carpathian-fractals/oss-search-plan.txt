ResearchForge OSS study plan

Public lead
- Reddit post: https://www.reddit.com/r/Fractalish/comments/1vxdukc/the_kind_of_raw_pure_majestic_mathematically/
- Reddit-hosted media: https://v.redd.it/7q7jb4t4qdlh1
- The author's public RSS comment identifies the renderer as MilkDrop3.

Authoritative leads to inspect
- MilkDrop3 source repository, README, release metadata, and license.
- ProjectM documentation and source history for independent descriptions of MilkDrop-compatible preset mechanics.
- Original or archived MilkDrop documentation describing coordinate feedback and preset equations.

Boundaries
- Architecture, mathematics, and public documentation references only.
- Do not clone, install, execute, translate, or import MilkDrop/MilkDrop3/projectM source or presets.
- Do not retain or ship the Reddit video, extracted frames, music, thumbnails, preset strings, textures, or screenshots.
- New visuals must be sampler-free, original GLSL and use only analytic geometry, deterministic iteration, and generated color.

Questions
1. Which structural mechanisms explain the observed isolated glowing rotating recursive objects?
2. Which mechanisms are generic mathematical ideas rather than protectable preset expression?
3. Which unused mathematical families fit Fractal Forge without duplicating its catalog?
4. Which parameter ranges are measurable, responsive, and performant on Flutter runtime effects?
5. What licensing and provenance facts must be recorded before implementation?
